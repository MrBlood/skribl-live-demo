# Cal's feedback on the Blooby brand and mascot ideas (Nov 24, 2026)

The owner kept these ideas as "fodder": "read it as ideas, not act on it,
but maybe give some feedback... i like the blooby peaking idea the most."
Nothing here is decided or on the build list. Any change on screen is a
mock first, and the owner's yes.

## Overall

The ideas are on the owner's side. Nothing in them fights what is already
decided about Blooby (`WORKING-AGREEMENTS.md`: he is hand-drawn, keep his
sloppiness, never glossy, and people's drawings matter more than he does).
The central idea, "a drawing that came to life", is the same idea as the
Pad's ("drawings that draw themselves"), which is why it fits.

## The peeking Blooby (the owner's favourite, and Cal's)

It says "curious, never interrupting", which is who he should be. To make
it work:

- Edges only. He never covers a drawing someone is working on (the canvas
  is the hero). He peeks from empty places:
  - an empty canvas before the first stroke, ducking away at the first
    touch;
  - an empty Gallery or Drafts tab;
  - the end of a feed;
  - the page for a wrong address.
- He needs room. A peek was tried once before, as the small Explore icon,
  and dropped: at that size his hands on the edge read as a little car
  with wheels (`DECISIONS.md`, "Explore's icon is Blooby holding up a
  bloob"). It works bigger, not as an icon.
- He is drawn by hand, ideally in the Pad, so his peek can replay, as his
  jump poses on the site's opening screen were drawn in the real Pad
  (`social/static/splash.js`).
- One Blooby per screen at most. Dark mode needs a look of its own, and
  Reduce Motion a still one.

## Other ideas worth keeping

- BLOOBY AS THE FIRST DRAWING PEOPLE DRAW BACK ON. This is the best idea
  here for the product. It shows Draw back off with the mascot, and it is
  the natural first weekly prompt in the final recommendations ("give
  Blooby a new expression"). The "flower grows in Flip" step needs Flip
  Draw back, which is on the owner's list.
- HE DRAWS HIMSELF. This is already the house style: the Blooby card in
  How it works draws itself and then he waves, and the opening screen
  writes "hello" while he jumps. Keep it rare and short: the opening
  screen's first show is already about 2.7 seconds, and it must never hold
  up a page.
- PERSONALITY WITHOUT WORDS (a happy hop, not a speech bubble), and NEVER A
  PERFECT ARTIST. The second is the owner's "keep his sloppiness" in other
  words.

## Taglines

- "Draw it. Watch it. Pass it on." is the one a first-time visitor would
  understand without being told. It is Cal's pick wherever the site
  explains itself.
- "Make a mark. See where it goes." is the lovelier feeling, and a good
  closing line.

Any change to words on screen is a mock first, and the owner's yes.
