How it works

Nothing matches that
Try loop, {% if is_flip %}onion{% else %}record{% endif %}, pressure or export.
{% if is_flip %}Flip is a frame-by-frame animator — draw a stack of pages, then flip through them. A one-page Flip is just a drawing; add pages to make it move.{% else %}Skribl Pad turns a drawing into a replay you can watch draw itself, with an optional music loop and background image, then post to a shareable link.{% endif %} Search above, or tap a section to open it.
{# ---- App workflow (app-specific) ---------------------------------- #}
{% if is_flip %}
1
Draw a page
Pick a color and brush size and sketch. The color dot opens the full Draw menu (colors, custom picker, eyedropper, brush size, background, opacity, smoothing). Need a different shape? The ⋯ menu switches the canvas between 4:3, 16:9, 1:1 and 9:16 — your drawings keep their position.
2
Add pages to animate
＋ Page copies the current page so you can nudge, erase, and redraw the difference; ＋ Blank starts empty. Turn on Onion skin to trace over the pages beneath — set how many show through (1–3) and tint them by distance so you can tell them apart. Hover a page in the strip to move, copy or hold it, or just drag it somewhere else in the strip.
3
Set the timing
Choose 6 / 12 / 24 frames per second in the header — that is the speed for the whole animation. To linger on one page, use its ×1 button in the strip to hold it for up to four beats; held pages show a badge so you can read the timing at a glance.
4
Music & image (optional)
Add a looping music track and a background image for the whole animation — see the Music and Image sections below.
5
Flip it & Post
Hit Flip it to preview the loop (drag the scrub bar to check any frame). When it looks right, tap Post to give it a title and caption, then get a link that plays for anyone, on any device.
{% else %}
1
Draw your canvas
Pick a color and brush size and sketch anything. Your first stroke on a blank canvas starts recording automatically — or hit Record first if you prefer.
2
Record the replay
Every stroke is captured with its timing, and Undo / Redo both work mid-take. Hit Stop when done — and you can hit Record again to add another take; the replay plays them back to back. Clear all in the ⋯ menu asks twice before it wipes anything, and offers an Undo afterwards that brings back your drawing, music and image.
3
Add music (optional)
Open the Music tab, add a track, and pick a loop — see the Music section below.
4
Add a background image (optional)
Open the Image tab to place an image behind your drawing — see the Image section below.
5
Preview and post
Hit Play to watch the replay with your music underneath. When it looks right, tap Post to give it a title and caption — the link works for anyone you share it with.
{% endif %}
{# ---- Drawing tools (shared) --------------------------------------- #}
Pen
Your main tool. Picking any color switches you back to the Pen, so you never erase by accident.{% if is_flip %} Shortcut: P.{% endif %}
Eraser
Removes parts of your drawing and reveals the background through it. A circle shows the exact area it clears — the eraser is 3× your brush size.{% if is_flip %} Shortcut: E.{% endif %}
Brush size
The slider sets the dot size you draw with; the eraser scales with it.
Pressure
Drawing with an Apple Pencil on iPad varies the line width as you press — harder is thicker, up to your brush size. A mouse or a finger draws at one width, so nothing changes for them. The eraser ignores pressure.
Color
Tap a swatch to draw with it, or the + dot to mix a custom color. Recent custom colors are kept between sessions for quick reuse.
Eyedropper
Pick any color already on the canvas to draw with it.
Background
Sets the color behind your whole drawing — not the strokes — and the eraser reveals it. Tap + for a custom background color.
Tips
Short one-time hints appear the first time you use something that needs explaining. Each shows once. Tips in the ⋯ menu turns them off — or back on, which also makes the ones you have already seen show again. It is one setting for Pad and Flip together, so a hint you have read on one will not reappear on the other.
Canvas
The ⋯ menu switches the canvas between 4:3, 16:9, 1:1 and 9:16.{% if is_flip %} Your drawings keep their position.{% else %} Pick it before you start — once you have drawn the canvas locks, so a resize cannot change the space your replay plays back into. Clear all unlocks it.{% endif %}
Opacity
Lowers how solid each new stroke is, for translucent layering. It bakes into the stroke, so set it before you draw.
Smoothing
Off / Low / High — evens out shaky lines as you draw.
Undo & clear
Ctrl/⌘+Z (or the undo button) removes your last stroke. Clear {{ 'all pages' if is_flip else 'drawing' }} asks for a second tap first, and the Undo beside it brings it back.{% if is_flip %} It clears pages only — your music and background image stay.{% endif %}
{# ---- Zoom & pan (shared) ------------------------------------------ #}
Magnifier
Toggles the zoom pill on the canvas. Zooming always centres, so to reach a particular spot, zoom in and then scroll or hold Space and drag. On touch, pinch zooms straight to where your fingers are. Turning it off returns you to 100%.
Zoom in / out
Use + / −, or tap the % to type an exact zoom. Fit returns to 100%.
Pinch
Pinch on a touchscreen to zoom (it turns the magnifier on for you); drag with two fingers to pan.
Pan (desktop)
While zoomed, scroll to pan, or hold Space and drag.
Move the controls
Drag the pill's grip to dock the zoom controls in whichever corner suits you.
{# ---- Frames & playback (Flip) / Recording & replay (Pad) ---------- #}
{% if is_flip %}
Pages
＋ Page copies the current page; ＋ Blank adds an empty one. Tap a page in the strip, or use ← / →, to move between them; a page's ✕ deletes it.
Onion skin
The stacked-sheets button in the header shows the pages beneath the one you are drawing, faintly, so you can line the next one up. Onion skin in the settings panel sets how many pages back to show, and the round toggle beside it tints them by distance.
Grid
In the Draw menu — overlays alignment guides across the canvas (a drawing aid only; never saved, hidden while flipping).
fps
6 / 12 / 24 frames per second sets how fast the pages play.
Draw-on
In the Draw menu — a playback style where each page redraws itself stroke-by-stroke, in the order you drew it, instead of snapping in. Works on a single page too.
Flip it & scrub
Plays the pages in a loop. A scrub bar over the canvas lets you drag to any frame. Shortcut: Space.
{% else %}
Auto-record
On a blank canvas, your first stroke arms recording automatically — or hit Record first.
Two timers
The badge shows how long your replay plays; recording captures the timing of each stroke, not the pauses.
Canvas locks between takes
A finished recording is locked so new pixels can't silently diverge from the replay. Undo/redo through recorded strokes still works.
Add more takes
Hit Record again to append another take; the replay plays them back to back.
Play & scrub
Play watches the replay with your music; drag the scrub bar to move through it.
{% endif %}
{# ---- Music & looping (shared) ------------------------------------- #}
Add a track
Open the Music tool and choose an audio file. One track plays under the whole {{ 'animation' if is_flip else 'replay' }}.
Loop markers
Drag the two handles on the waveform to pick the slice that loops.
Move the loop
Drag inside the selection to slide the whole loop window without resizing it.
Loop Detail
Zoom into the seam for frame-accurate trimming; scroll to move along the waveform.
Crossfade
Folds the loop's tail over its head so the wrap is seamless — the export bakes it in.
Preview Loop
Plays your selection on repeat — gapless, so you hear exactly how it will loop.
Test Seam
Plays across the loop point a couple of times so you can judge (and smooth) the join.
Nudge
The fine controls shift the trim points by tiny amounts for a perfect loop.
{# ---- Background image (shared) ------------------------------------ #}
Add an image
Open the Image tool and choose a photo to sit behind your drawing.
Fill / Fit / Stretch
Choose how the image frames the canvas — crop to fill, fit inside, or stretch to the edges.
Reposition
Drag to move the image within the frame (in Fill mode).
Zoom
Scale the image up inside the frame.
Opacity
Fade the image back so your strokes stand out.
Blur
Soften the image into a background wash.
Reset
Clearing the canvas never removes the image; Reset returns the adjustments to default.
{# ---- Save, export & sharing (shared + conditional) ---------------- #}
Autosave
Your work is saved in this browser as you go and restored when you come back.
Drafts
The ⋯ menu can Save / Load a .skribl file to move work between devices.
Export
{% if is_flip %}From the ⋯ menu: a PNG of the current page, a GIF of the loop, or a video with your music. The video button names the format your browser will actually produce — MP4 where it can, WebM otherwise. Under GIF you can choose a Solid or Transparent background. Size and Pages apply to the video and the GIF — a PNG is always the current page at full size. Loops is video only: it repeats the animation so a short clip is not a one-second file, and the note under it says how long the result will be. A GIF ignores it because a GIF loops forever on its own.{% else %}Export a PNG of your drawing, a GIF of the replay, or a video with your music. The video button names the format your browser will actually produce — MP4 where it can, WebM otherwise.{% endif %}
Post
Give it a title and an optional caption, then publish to a shareable link that plays your {{ 'animation' if is_flip else 'Skribl' }} — with loop, mute, and music — for anyone, on any device.
Your Skribls
The ⋯ menu keeps a list of everything you have posted from this browser, so you can find a link again after closing the tab. It is saved in this browser only — it is not an account, and clearing site data removes it. Removing a row removes the row, not the Skribl.
Your Skribls
The ⋯ menu keeps a list of everything you have posted, so a link is never lost when you close the tab. It lives in this browser only — it is not an account, and clearing site data clears it.
Report a problem
Copies your version, browser and canvas details so a bug report says something useful. Your drawing, titles and audio are not included.
Restore on reload
Reopening the app brings your last {{ unit }} back automatically.
