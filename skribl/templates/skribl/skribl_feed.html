This is the in-post player. Tap a Skribl to play it: the drawing redraws itself, a hairline runs along the bottom edge, and the nib shows where the pen is. Two controls, bottom-left: sound, off until you turn it on and then on for the whole page, and loop, on until you turn it off — which leaves the drawing finished and stops the music with it. Nothing else: the full transport lives on the shared link at /s/<id>, not in every post.

Only one plays at a time, and scrolling one out of view settles it. Nothing is fetched until you tap: an idle post is one cached image.

A host embeds it in two lines: {% raw %}{{ skribl_inline_assets() }}{% endraw %} once per page and {% raw %}{{ skribl_inline(id) }}{% endraw %} once per post — see docs/INTEGRATION.md.
