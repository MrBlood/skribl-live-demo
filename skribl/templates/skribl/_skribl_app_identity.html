{# THE APP'S IDENTITY TO A BROWSER AND A HOME SCREEN (SK-AUD-013; acquisition audit of v302). Until this partial the principal pages carried no manifest, no icon and no theme colour, so a Skribl added to a phone's Home Screen got a screenshot for an icon and browser-default chrome around it. One include, four pages -- Pad, Flip, the player, the library -- and NOT the feed, which is a host's page that is not Skribl and must not claim to be. The manifest is a ROUTE rather than a static file so its URLs follow the blueprint's mount point through url_for, like every other path Skribl emits. The icons are Blooby waving on a lilac tile (v320, the owner's pick of the mocks), drawn from his own strokes by `harness/tools/blooby.py icon skribl/static`; regenerate them that way rather than editing the PNGs. verify_identity reads the tile's colour from blooby.py and checks the pixels. The theme colour is the dark ground here, and the two grounds ride on the tag as data- attributes so the theme boot (before first paint) and lib/theme.js (on a switch) can re-stamp it without holding a colour of their own: the browser's chrome follows the PAGE's theme, not the OS's alone. skribl.core.THEME_GROUND is the one place the two values live in Python, and verify_identity.py pins them against the stylesheet's own --surface-base in both ramps. Include this BEFORE _skribl_theme_boot.html, which looks the meta up as it runs. #} {# OPENING WITH NO SIGNAL (lib/offline.js, the /sw.js route): the same pages that can be added to a Home Screen can be opened from it on a train. The worker answers from its copy only when the network fails, so a phone with a signal always gets the newest page. #}
