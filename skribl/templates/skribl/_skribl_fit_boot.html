{# THE CANVAS IN ITS PLACE FROM THE FIRST FRAME (the calm open, part 2; the owner: "I want pad and flip to be the same... it has to be subtle cuz it happens a lot", then "Yes calm"). Flip used to open on a 300x150 box that jumped to full size when its scripts were done; the Pad's canvas settled (a 9:16 guess 26px too tall on a phone; on a desk a 4:3 guess that turned into a square). _skribl_fit_early.html, right after each editor's column, now lays the canvas out before anything paints, with the editor's own sums. What it needs from the two libraries those sums use comes HERE, in the head, before the column is read: lib/canvassizes.js (which shape a new drawing starts in) and lib/chromefit.js (the row's reach on a wide window; and the watch that re-fits the canvas when the chrome changes size, window or no window). They are small and define globals only; render-blocking here, they arrive with the stylesheets. The editors' deferred scripts find them already there. Until it has been laid out, the canvas is not shown, so a slow line that paints the page in pieces never shows it at a size it will leave; should the early fit never run, it shows on its own at 4 s (and each editor's boot marks it laid out too). The letter sheet keeps its own layout. #}
