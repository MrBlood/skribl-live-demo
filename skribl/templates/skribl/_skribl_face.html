{# THE FACE PLAYER: a skribl in a circle, as somebody's picture (after v330, the owner's "all yes" on phase 1's mocks: "Faces that draw themselves: everyone's picture is a skribl they draw of themselves; it draws itself once, in about four seconds (at 4×), when someone opens their profile, and sits still everywhere else"). {% from 'skribl/_skribl_face.html' import skribl_face_assets, skribl_face %} {{ skribl_face_assets() }} once per page, in ... {{ skribl_face(face_id, canvas_w=w, canvas_h=h) }} still {{ skribl_face(face_id, play='once', canvas_w=w, canvas_h=h, alt="Mira's face, drawn by Mira") }} draws itself A SMALL PIECE OF ITS OWN, NOT THE IN-POST PLAYER WITH ITS CONTROLS HIDDEN. A face has no transport, no line, no play button, no sound and no nib: it is a picture that, once, draws itself. So it has its own macro, stylesheet (face.css) and script (lib/face.js), and the in-post player's budget (skribl_inline_assets, verify_inline) is untouched. What a face borrows is the player's ENGINE: its insides come from the player's own macro (skribl_inline_draft with chrome=false: the parts the engine needs and none it shows), and lib/face.js hands the drawing to SkriblInline.attach(), so a face replays with its own timing, pressure and colours, exactly as a post and /s/ do. A page with a face that draws itself loads skribl_inline_assets() too (in either order); a still face needs nothing but this file's markup and stylesheet. STILL ('still', the default): the face skribl's own poster (/s//poster, the share card), framed so the drawing's middle square fills the circle. One cached image, no script, no fetch, no play counted. The frame is worked out here from the drawing's size (canvas_w and canvas_h, the post's own columns, which create_post hands back on made.post), with lib/sharecard.js drawingRect()'s sums and rounding, and written as three custom properties face.css reads. Without the size it takes a square, the shape the Pad's face mode draws on (?compose=1&face=1). ONCE ('once'): the still picture waits unseen under it, and over it the face draws itself: lib/face.js fetches the drawing (GET /api/skribls/, which counts a play, as any player's fetch does) and plays it once, at `speed` (4×), or faster where that would take longer than `most` seconds (5: "about four seconds", the owner's words, with room for a face drawn in twenty), so a face drawn for an hour is still a hello. It starts from the empty circle, the drawing fades in on its first frame, and it rests finished. UNDER REDUCE MOTION it never moves: the still picture is the face from the first paint and nothing is fetched. If the drawing cannot be had, the still picture fades in where it would have drawn; with no script at all, after a moment on its own (face.css). `size` is the circle's width in px, written on the face; leave it out and the page's CSS sizes it (--skribl-face-size, 40px unsaid), which is how a page gives one face two sizes at two widths. `alt` names the face for a screen reader (role=img); without it the face is decoration, for a face beside a name that already says whose it is. A still face is a span, so it can sit in a link or a button; one that draws itself is a block. EVERY URL IS BUILT FROM AN ABSOLUTE ENDPOINT NAME, as the player's are, so a host renders this from its own views under any url_prefix; `bp` is the blueprint's name for a host that renamed it. #} {% from 'skribl/_skribl_inline_player.html' import skribl_inline_draft %} {% macro skribl_face_assets() -%} {%- endmacro %} {# The drawing's middle square inside the share card, as the poster's frame: lib/sharecard.js drawingRect() (the card 1200 x 630, its area 1092 x 492, the footer 84), its Math.round as x + 0.5 floored (every term here is positive), then the square, 3 card pixels in from the plate's hairline.
