{# Whether the hello comes, decided BEFORE FIRST PAINT, and its ground laid over the page when it does (lib/hello.js, _skribl_hello.html). The owner, on a reload: "it goes to the canvas first, then goes to the skribl cursive with i's dot dropping in." The hello waits for the page's own scripts and its files before it shows, so the page painted the canvas first and the hello came in over it. Decided here, inline in as the theme is (_skribl_theme_boot.html, for the same reason), the first paint is the hello's own ground and the canvas is first seen as the hello leaves. THE DECISION IS MADE HERE ONLY. lib/hello.js reads data-hello-comes and decides nothing of its own; the key it marks seen with is this one (verify_hello reads the two for the same key). It comes when this browser has never seen this hello, on a page of its own (not a composer's frame); ?hello=again plays it on any open, seen or not; automation never meets it unless it asks (window.__skriblHelloForce, set before the page loads). THE GROUND NEVER HOLDS UP A PAGE THAT IS READY. It stays while the page's own scripts come, as an app's launch screen does (the canvas does nothing before they run); once they are done, lib/hello.js waits 0.3 s more for the hello's files and then fades it, and the hello, if its files come later, comes in over the canvas as it used to. If lib/hello.js never runs, or the page never gets that far, it fades by itself at 8 s. data-hello-cover is "up" while it waits, "on" once the hello is over it, "lift" as it fades (styles.css, THE HELLO'S GROUND). Any failure leaves both off: no hello, no ground, the page as it was. #}
