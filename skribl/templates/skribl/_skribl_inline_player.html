WHY THE MARKUP IS HERE AND NOT BUILT BY THE SCRIPT. inlineplayer.js could create all of this from one

Server-rendered, an unenhanced post is still the drawing's share card with a play affordance on it — a still image of the Skribl, which is a truthful degradation. The script only ever adds behaviour to markup that already says something. THE CANVAS CARRIES NO SIZE, deliberately.
