How Flip Mode works

Draw a frame-by-frame animation, then flip through the pages.

Draw
Sketch on the page. The colour dot opens the full draw menu — the same one as the Skribl Pad: colour presets, a + custom picker, an eyedropper, recent colours, brush size, background colour, smoothing, and opacity. Switch between Pen and Eraser on the tool row.
Image & Music
The image and music buttons on the tool row each open a drawer: add a background image that fills the canvas, or a music loop that plays while you Flip (one of each, for the whole animation).
Add pages
＋ Page copies the current page so you can tweak it; ＋ Blank starts empty.
Grid
The grid button overlays fine / medium / coarse alignment guides across the whole canvas — a drawing aid only (never saved, hidden while flipping).
Onion skin
The onion toggle shows the previous page faintly underneath, so you can trace the motion.
Flip it
Plays the pages in a loop at your chosen speed — 6 / 12 / 24 frames per second.
Move around
Tap a page in the strip, or use ← / → to step between pages.
Undo
Ctrl/⌘ + Z or the undo button removes your last stroke.
Delete
A page's ✕ removes that page; Clear animation (in the draw menu) wipes the whole thing. Both need a second tap (they turn red) to confirm.
More (⋯)
The ⋯ menu holds Save / Load draft (as a .skribl file) and Export — a PNG of the current page or a WebM of the animation.
Autosave
Your animation is saved in this browser and restored when you come back.
Each new page is a copy of the current one — nudge, erase, redraw to animate. Toggle Onion to see the frame beneath. Then hit Flip it.
