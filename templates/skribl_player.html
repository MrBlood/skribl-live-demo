How it works
Everything you can do in Skribl Pad, grouped by area. Tap a section to open it.
1
Draw your canvas
Pick a color and brush size and sketch anything. Draw before you record to set a starting image, or start from a blank canvas — either works.
2
Record the replay
Hit Record and every new stroke is captured in real time. Undo and Redo both work while recording. Hit Stop when you're done — your Skribl plays back exactly as you drew it.
3
Add music (optional)
Open the Music tab and add a track, then drag the loop markers to pick up to 20 seconds. Preview Loop plays your selection on repeat; Test Seam checks the join sounds smooth.
4
Add a background photo (optional)
Open the Photo tab to place an image behind your drawing. Choose Fill, Fit, or Stretch and fade it back with opacity. Clearing the canvas never removes the photo.
5
Preview and post
Hit Play to watch your replay with the music underneath, and Stop any time. When it looks right, tap Post to give it a title and caption and get a player link. When you post, your Skribl is saved on the server and the link works for anyone you share it with, on any device. If the server can’t be reached, it’s kept on this device only and the app tells you so.
Pen
Your main drawing tool. Selecting a color automatically switches you back to the Pen.
Eraser
Removes parts of your drawing. A circle follows your cursor showing the exact eraser size — the eraser is 3× your current brush size, so it clears more with each pass.
Brush size
The four circles show the actual dot size you'll draw with. The active one highlights in purple.
More tools
The tool row stays minimal — tap More tools to open color, background, and brush options. The colored dot on the button shows your current color at a glance. Tap again to collapse.
Color
Inside More tools, tap any swatch to draw with it, or the + dot for a custom color. Your recent custom colors appear in a Recent row for quick reuse.
Background
The Background swatches (in More tools) change the color behind your drawing — not the drawing itself. Tap + for a custom background color.
Opacity
Lowers how solid each stroke is, for translucent layering. Set it before you draw — it bakes into the stroke.
Smoothing
Off / Low / High steadies shaky lines as you draw — especially handy on a phone. It only affects new strokes.
Eyedropper
Pick a color from anywhere on screen (or, in some browsers, tap the canvas) and it becomes your pen color.
Undo / Redo
Step back and forward stroke by stroke. Both work while recording. Redo history clears if you draw something new.
Canvas locks
Once you finish a recording, the canvas locks so your replay stays exactly as captured — you can't draw over a finished Skribl. To change it, hit Record to start a new take (it keeps the current drawing as its starting image), or Clear to begin from blank.
Play time
While recording you'll see two timers: clock time and play time. Long pauses between strokes are shortened on playback, so a drawing with big gaps plays back faster than the clock — play time is how long the finished replay actually runs.
Stop playback
During replay the Play button becomes Stop — tap it any time to end the preview early.
Add a track
Tap the Music dropzone to browse, or drag an audio file straight onto it. MP3, M4A, WAV, OGG, and AAC all work.
Loop markers
Drag the two handles on the waveform to pick the section that plays under your drawing — up to 20 seconds. The selected range highlights in purple.
Move the loop
Grab the middle of the purple selection and drag to slide the whole loop to another part of the song, keeping its length. Pair it with Match Drawing to audition the same duration over different sections.
Loop Detail
Zooms in on your selection so you can place the start and end points precisely, with a moving playhead while you preview. Use the focus buttons (Loop / Start / End) and the magnifier (1×–8×) to zoom right up to a boundary; the Start / End / Step fine-tune controls sit just below.
Match Drawing
Snaps the loop length to match how long your drawing replay runs, so the music and the drawing finish together. It resizes in place, keeping your current start point.
Fine-tune
The Start and End − / + buttons nudge each loop edge, with the current time shown between them. Use the Step control to make each nudge finer or coarser, down to a hundredth of a second.
Preview Loop
Plays your selected section on repeat so you can hear exactly what will loop under the drawing.
Test Seam
Plays the last moment of your loop, then jumps to the start — so you can hear whether the join is smooth before posting.
Add a photo
Tap the Photo dropzone to browse, or drag an image onto it. JPG, PNG, GIF, and WebP are supported.
Fill / Fit / Stretch
Fill covers the whole canvas (cropping if needed), Fit shows the whole image inside it, and Stretch forces it to the exact canvas shape.
Reposition
In Fill, part of your photo is cropped. Tap Reposition photo, then drag the image to choose which part shows behind your drawing.
Zoom
Also in Fill, the Zoom slider enlarges the photo inside the canvas — then reposition to frame exactly the part you want.
Opacity
Fade the photo back so your drawing stands out on top of it.
Blur
Soften the photo so it reads as a backdrop and your strokes pop on top. Slide it back to zero for a sharp image.
Reset adjustments
Puts fit, opacity, blur, position, and zoom back to their defaults without removing the photo.
More menu
The ··· button in the top bar holds Save draft, Load draft, Export, and Clear canvas.
Export
Save your Skribl as a PNG image or a WebM video — the stroke replay with your looped music — to share outside the app.
Save draft
Saves everything — your drawing, background, photo, and music with its loop points — as a .skribl file you can reopen and keep editing later.
Load draft
Reopens a saved .skribl file, restoring the drawing, photo, and music exactly as you left them. A loaded replay opens locked, just like a finished recording.
Clear canvas
Wipes the drawing and recording so you can start over. Your background photo stays put. This can't be undone once done, so save a draft first if you might want it back.
When you Post, your Skribl is saved on the server and the player link works for anyone, on any device. If the server can’t be reached, posting falls back to saving on this device only — and the app says “Saved on this device only” instead of claiming it posted.
Post
Opens a sheet to give your Skribl a title and an optional caption, with a preview of the drawing. Posting saves it to the server and hands back a player link you can share (with a device-only fallback if the server can’t be reached).
Watch your Skribl
After posting, tap Watch your Skribl to open it in the player — the same replay, presented on its own with no editing tools.
Player controls
Play starts the replay and becomes Pause, which freezes it in place; tap again to carry on. Restart plays from the beginning, and the loop toggle replays it automatically. If the Skribl has music, a mute button lets you silence it.
Copy link
Copies the player link. Share it with anyone — it opens the posted Skribl for them too. (For a device-only fallback save, the link only opens on this device.)
