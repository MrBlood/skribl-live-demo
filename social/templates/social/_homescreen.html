Add to Home Screen

BloobyOpens full screen, like an app
  1. 1Tap ⋯More in Safari’s bar, then Share
  2. 2Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
  3. 3Keep Open as Web App on, and tap Add

Open it from your Home Screen. It may ask you to sign in once there, with your passkey.
