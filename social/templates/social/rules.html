A draft, in plain words. A lawyer reads it before Blooby opens to everyone.

{% if page == 'terms' %}

Your drawings are yours

You keep everything you make. By posting, you let Blooby show it where you chose: in your post, on Home, and on Explore if you tick it. Delete it and it goes.

Who can join

People 13 and older, by invite for now. One account each, as yourself.

Be kind

No harassment, hate, or threats. Nothing that puts a child at risk, ever. The community rules say more, with examples.

When something is reported

A person looks at it. If it breaks the rules it comes down, and we tell its maker why.

{% elif page == 'privacy' %}

What we keep

Your name, your handle, what you write about yourself, and your face if you draw one. Your posts, your comments and your drawings, and where you chose to show each. Who invited you, and when you said you’re 13 or older.

For signing in, the public half of each passkey and when it was last used. There are no passwords, and nothing kept here can sign in as you.

What we don’t do

No ads, and nothing sold. Everything on these pages comes from Blooby itself, so nobody else watches what you do here. Two cookies: one keeps you signed in, the other keeps your forms safe.

To stop floods of sign-ups or reports, we count tries by a scrambled form of you or your address, never the address itself.

Who sees what

Anyone can see your profile and what you share. A drawing you keep to yourself stays yours. When you report something, only the people who run Blooby see that it was you. When you block someone, they aren’t told. Something taken down for breaking the rules is hidden from everybody but its maker; it isn’t deleted.

When you leave

Delete your account and your profile, posts and comments go with it, words and drawings both. Your other drawings and drafts stay until you delete them in your Library, and the page that deletes your account says so first. A report that named you stays, without your name.

{% else %}

Blooby is for drawing, and for being kind about each other’s drawings. These are the few rules, with an example of each.

Be kind

No harassment, hate or threats. Saying what you’d change is fine; putting someone down isn’t. “I’d love it in brighter colors” is kind. “This is terrible” under someone’s post is not.

Keep children safe

Nothing that sexualizes a child or puts a child at risk, ever. It comes down at once, and we report it as the law requires.

Be yourself

One account each, as yourself, and you have to be 13 or older. Don’t pretend to be someone else, and don’t post the same thing over and over to be seen.

Share what you made

Post your own drawings. If you traced or built on someone else’s, say whose. Posting someone else’s drawing as yours isn’t allowed.

If something breaks the rules

Report it from its ⋯ menu. A person looks at it; if it breaks the rules it comes down, and its maker is told why. You can block anyone too: you won’t see each other, and they aren’t told.
